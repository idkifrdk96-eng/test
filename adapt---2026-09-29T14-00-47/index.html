THE DIAL
Top-left, read outward: the green core is your health (it comes back a few seconds after you are left alone; bottom out and you go down and get back up on the spot with the meters wiped), the three red pills are skills 1 / 2 / 3 (a pill shortens as its cooldown runs down; a press while it is cold flashes it), the cyan ring is the ultimate (it pulses when it is ready to fire), the purple diamond beside the dial is the air combo's own cooldown (it fills from the bottom as it comes back, and pulses when a dive would launch a body that is already in the air — and it glows while a combo is actually running), and the gold letters are your STYLE rank, climbing D → C → B → A → S → SS → SSS as you land hits, win clashes and block. Variety pays: a move you did not just throw, and anything that is not the plain M1 string, is worth more. Getting hit drops the rank two or three letters; being knocked out wipes it.
TIPS
Speed builds up: you start at a slow pace and accelerate to top speed the longer you hold a direction, so release the keys to drop back down when you need to pick your way across something fiddly. Momentum: jump the instant you land (a perfect chain) to gain speed, and speed carries through the air — a dive builds speed while it holds, and diving into a slide keeps every bit of it, then wall-jump off a wall to whip around the corner with it. Run into a wall (or hold toward it) and you stick to it: steer along the face, wall-jump off it, or just JUMP at it and press-and-hold JUMP again in the air and he CLIMBS — with a STAMINA bar that runs down into a slip — climbs hug the wall, pull you over the top of a short ledge automatically, and wall-jump between two walls to gain height. A running jump that clips a wall beside you turns into a wall run — steer along the face and leap off before it runs out. Dive straight at a wall and hit M1 (left click) as it lands and you boot off it instead: the camera spins a full turn onto your new line and the dive comes out of it faster than it went in — and the boot works out of any air, so a plain jump at a wall kicks just as well (it just leaves you at the speed you brought). The boot is forgiving: a wall within half a unit of your line counts, almost any angle short of "behind me" counts, and a click a blink before or after you touch still lands. Slam down from a tower and hit JUMP on impact for a big bounce, or hold a direction to slide straight out of it. Slide through anything standing in your way and it goes off its feet — the faster the slide, the higher it is thrown. And a hard landing is not only your own thud: come down off a roof and the shock throws everyone standing around you into the air, harder and wider the further you fell. Slamming is also how you take the baseplate apart: one boot cracks the surface where you hit, and if you keep putting them in the same spot the cracks spread, then the surface comes apart — a block breaks off into loose polygonal chunks, and the ground sinks into a crater you can stand in and work deeper.
